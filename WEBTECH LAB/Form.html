School Kits

We provide books, uniforms, shoes, and stationery to children from low-income families, covering their full academic year so that cost is never the reason a child misses school.

Mid-Day Meals

Every day, we serve a nutritious, freshly cooked meal to children in our partner schools, so no child has to study, play, or grow on an empty stomach.

Health Camps

Our medical teams travel to rural villages to run free health check-up camps, offering basic diagnostics, consultations, and medicines to families with little access to healthcare.

Disaster Relief

When floods, cyclones, or other emergencies strike, we deliver emergency food rations, clean water, and essential supplies directly to affected families within hours.

Elderly Care Kits

We support senior citizens living alone or in low-income households with essential care kits covering nutrition, hygiene, and basic medical needs for three months at a time.
